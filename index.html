Focus Mirror

Fog up your display using your microphone, then interact using hand gestures.

Local processing only. Your camera and microphone data are processed entirely within this browser tab and never leave your device.

breathe · pinch · wipe

  • 01Breathe

    Blow softly into your microphone to generate fog on the screen.

  • 02Pinch

    Touch your thumb and index finger together to draw and clear the fog.

  • 03Fist

    Close your hand and swipe to clear a wide patch of the canvas.
